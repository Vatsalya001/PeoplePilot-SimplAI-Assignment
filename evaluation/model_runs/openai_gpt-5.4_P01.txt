Answer:
For a full-time employee, the policy states 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year. These are credited at 1.5 days per completed month, so the leave accrues monthly rather than being fully granted upfront. Therefore, no, all 18 days are not credited on 1 January.

Conditions and Exceptions:

The policy passage provided says annual leave is credited per completed month.
It also expressly states that the annual entitlement does not mean all 18 days are credited at the beginning of the year.
The excerpt does not distinguish a different annual-leave entitlement for another employee category in this section, but your question specifies a full-time employee and the cited rule applies to employees generally in the leave section.

Section 3.1 – Annual leave entitlement and accrual
“Employees receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month.”
“Twelve completed monthly credits total 18 days.”
“The annual entitlement does not mean that all 18 days are credited at the beginning of the year.”
