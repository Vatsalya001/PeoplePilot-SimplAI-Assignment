Annual Leave — Full-Time Employees at AsterByte Technologies
Answer
Question	Policy Answer
How many days per calendar year?	18 paid annual leave days
How are they credited?	1.5 days per completed month (12 months × 1.5 = 18 days)
Are all 18 days credited on 1 January?	No. Days accrue monthly as each month is completed.
Conditions and Exceptions
Monthly accrual model: Leave is earned as each calendar month is completed — an employee does not have access to the full 18 days on 1 January.
Manager approval required: Annual leave must be approved by the employee's manager. Requests should normally be submitted at least five working days in advance.
Carry-forward: Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry forward to the following year, expiring on 31 March of that year.
Probation / joining-year specifics: The policy notes that probation-specific leave arrangements exist; employees should consult HR for details applicable to their situation.
Source

"Employees receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month. Twelve completed monthly credits total 18 days. The annual entitlement does not mean that all 18 days are credited at the beginning of the year."
