Answer: Full-time employees at AsterByte receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year, which are credited at a rate of 1.5 days for each completed month. This means that the total of 18 annual leave days is not credited all at once on January 1 but rather accrued monthly over the year.

Conditions and Exceptions: Annual leave requires manager approval, and employees are expected to request leave at least five working days in advance when possible.
