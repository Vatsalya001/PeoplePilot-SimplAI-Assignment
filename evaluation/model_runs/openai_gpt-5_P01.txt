Answer:

Annual leave days per calendar year (full-time): 18 paid days.
How they are credited: Accrued at 1.5 days per completed month (12 completed months = 18 days).
Are all days credited on 1 January?: No. The policy states the annual entitlement does not mean all 18 days are credited at the beginning of the year.

Conditions and Exceptions:

Applies to full-time employees.
Leave usage requires manager approval; normal request lead time is at least five working days.
